Before we start

A few useful details.

Are these fixed prices?

These are starting prices for the packages described above. Complexity, content, integrations and your existing setup affect the final quote. Work starts after we agree the scope.

Is yearly maintenance required?

No. Website maintenance is optional, priced per year and billed separately from the initial build. We’ll confirm that your website fits the maintenance scope before starting.

Yes. Share the website or a description of your application and the changes you need. I’ll review the scope and provide a quote for the work.
